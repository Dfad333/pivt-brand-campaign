# PivtMedia — Campaign Reference Accounts

Two saved inspiration sources. They are **different systems**, not variations of one look —
use them deliberately rather than blending by default.

---

## 1. BOEGÖW — @boegow · boegow.com · "Connecting What Matters."

Brutalist Swiss **editorial poster** design. Type is the hero; photography is an element.

**System**
- Huge condensed grotesque headline (Helvetica/Neue Haas register), broken over two lines,
  dominating the lower half. Short declarative sentences ending in periods —
  "STRIPPED BACK." / "DIALED IN." / "CONNECTING WHAT MATTERS"
- B&W photography as a **cropped block or full-bleed element**, always subordinate to type.
  Frequently a grainy abstract blur instead of a subject.
- ONE neon accent (their green) as a **solid geometric shape** — square, circle, rotated
  tape — often with the wordmark sitting inside it.
- Clusters of tiny 6–8pt micro-copy floating in corners and over the image
  ("ENOUGH CLARITY." · "WE DESIGN SO PEOPLE DON'T HAVE TO THINK ABOUT DESIGN.")
- Hairline grid rules; bracketed fragments — "[ a new dawn ]"
- Small wordmark bottom-centre

**Maps to PivtMedia:** their neon green → `#FF5C00`. The barcode mark reads as native here
(technical, annotation-flavoured). Local images saved in `boegow/`.

## 2. LONGEBLACK — @longeblack · longeblack.com

"Strategic Luxury Brand Management Firm by @lxias. Based in Cairo with Successful Brands
Worldwide. #EgyptsFinest". A **warm luxury** system — nearly the opposite of BOEGÖW.

**System**
- Cinematic, richly-lit editorial photography — amber, honey-gold, caramel, deep chocolate
  shadow. Saturated and warm, never cool or desaturated.
- Centred, **widely letterspaced LIGHT/THIN sans** headline — usually the brand name
  ("CUNACCI" · "HIGHSTREET" · "THE HANGARD™"). Restrained, small relative to the frame.
- Trademark marks used deliberately — ™ and Ⓡ
- A discreet endorsement lockup beneath the headline — "A BRAND BY LONGEBLACK"
- Luxury interiors, fashion, events; people present but atmospheric
- Copy voice is **exclusivity**: "Not for everyone. And that's the point." ·
  "Curated People .. Timeless Energy" · "Ready for what's next?"

**Maps to PivtMedia:** `#FF5C00` already lives in this amber world. The endorsement lockup
translates directly to positioning PivtMedia as the firm behind a client's brand.

## Choosing between them

| Use BOEGÖW when… | Use LONGEBLACK when… |
|---|---|
| Making a statement / manifesto post | Presenting a client brand or case study |
| The message is the product | The atmosphere is the product |
| B&W, stark, loud type | Warm, cinematic, quiet type |
| PivtMedia speaking as itself | PivtMedia endorsing someone else's work |
